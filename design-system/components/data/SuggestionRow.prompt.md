The inline-first correction affordance: suggested shot, its confidence, accept (`Enter`) or correct (`O`).

```jsx
<SuggestionRow shot="Smash" confidence={0.61} time="12:04.120" onAccept={accept} onCorrect={openPanel} />
```

Dashed lime border marks "not yet a fact". It sits at the bottom of the stroke feed and never blocks playback.
