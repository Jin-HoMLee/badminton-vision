The full 11-class shuttle-insights taxonomy: Serve, Clear, Drop, Smash, Half Smash, Lift, Net Shot, Net Kill, Push, Drive, Block.

```jsx
<ShotPicker value={shot} suggested="Smash" onChange={setShot} columns={4} />
```

Keys `1–9` label the first nine and are printed on the buttons. A suggestion is dashed + lime text; the moment the user picks, it becomes a solid lime selection with manual provenance.
